你在 App 裡輸入的資料都存在你的裝置上；只有在你開啟 iCloud 同步時，才會另外存到你自己的 iCloud。我們不收集、不追蹤，也沒有廣告。

1. 你的資料在你的裝置與你自己的 iCloud

生日簿的姓名與生日、共時記的天氣、心情、共時與文字、調頻紀錄，以及各項設定，都儲存在你的裝置上（包括主畫面小工具），不會上傳給我們或任何第三方。

iCloud 同步（iPhone、iPad；預設關閉）：你在「設定 › iCloud 同步」選擇「即時同步」後，上述資料會透過 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit 與 iCloud 鍵值儲存）存到你自己 Apple ID 的私人 iCloud 空間，並同步到登入同一個 Apple ID 的其他裝置。這些資料只有你能存取，我們看不到、也無法讀取，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策。是否開啟通知等只屬於單一裝置的設定不會同步。

同步開啟時，在一台裝置刪除的內容會在所有裝置上一起刪除。改回「關閉」後，這台裝置會停止同步；已存在 iCloud 的資料會保留，直到你刪除。

  • iCloud（Apple）：只有在你開啟 iCloud 同步時才會連線，見第 1 節。
  • App Store／Google Play：購買由 App Store／Google Play 處理，我們拿不到你的付款資料。

iPhone、iPad：使用 iOS 內建語音，在裝置上進行。Android：只使用手機上已安裝的離線語音，在裝置上進行。

App 不使用廣告、追蹤或第三方分析工具，也不會跨 App 或網站追蹤你。

你可以在 App 內刪除生日簿人物或共時記內容；刪除 App 會一併刪除裝置上的所有資料。若曾開啟 iCloud 同步，iCloud 中的資料可在 iPhone 的「設定 › 你的名字 › iCloud › 管理帳號儲存空間」找到 13 月亮曆並刪除。

6. 兒童

App 不會向任何人（包括兒童）收集個人資料。

13 Moon Calendar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026
Provided by SABEE ABUNDANCE CO. LTD. (沙比豐盛心靈有限公司)

Everything you enter in the app is stored on your device, and in your own iCloud only if you turn on iCloud Sync. We don't collect it, we don't track you, and there are no ads.

1. Your data stays on your device and in your own iCloud

Names and birthdays in People, the weather, moods, synchronicities and notes in your journal, your Tune In sessions, and your settings are stored on your device (including the Home Screen widget). They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

iCloud Sync (iPhone and iPad; off by default): when you choose “Live sync” in Settings › iCloud Sync, this data is stored through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage) in the private iCloud space of your own Apple ID, and synced to your other devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Only you can access it; we cannot see or read it. Apple's privacy policy applies. Settings that belong to one device, such as whether notifications are on, are not synced.

While sync is on, anything you delete on one device is deleted on all of them. When you switch back to “Off”, this device stops syncing; data already in iCloud stays there until you delete it.

2. What the app connects to

  • iCloud (Apple): only when you turn on iCloud Sync; see section 1.
  • App Store / Google Play: purchases are handled by the App Store / Google Play; we never receive your payment details.
  • SoundOn (soundon.fm): the chant recordings are published there as a podcast. When you tap “Chant”, the app streams it from SoundOn, and at most once a day downloads the podcast’s public episode list. SoundOn sees your IP address and which episode is played (the KIN you are viewing); the request contains no name, birthday or journal text. SoundOn’s privacy policy applies. The website link in Settings opens sabee.space in your browser.
  • The app never connects to our servers. Links to our website, podcast, Instagram, YouTube and this policy open in your browser.
  • sabee.space (app 1.2 and earlier): these versions download the podcast list from it. Like any server, it sees your IP address and basic connection information, which are used only to provide the service, never to identify or track you. The request contains no name, birthday or journal text.

3. Read aloud

iPhone and iPad: uses the voices built into iOS, on your device. Android: uses only the offline voices installed on your phone, on your device.

4. No tracking, no analytics, no ads

The app has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete people or journal entries in the app; deleting the app deletes all of its data on the device. If you have used iCloud Sync, you can delete the app's data in iCloud from Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage, under 13 Moon Calendar.

6. Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

7. The Android app

  • Works without internet: the app works fully offline. It connects to SoundOn only when you tap “Chant” to stream.
  • What the app connects to: SoundOn only
    • When you tap “Chant”, the app streams it from SoundOn (soundon.fm), and at most once a day downloads the podcast’s public episode list.
    • SoundOn sees your IP address and which episode is played (the KIN you are viewing); the request contains no name, birthday or journal text. SoundOn’s privacy policy applies.
    • The chants for KIN 150 and 151 are built into the app and need no connection.
    • The app connects to nothing else, including our servers. The links in Settings to our website, podcast, Instagram, YouTube and this policy open in your browser.
  • Your data stays on your device: People, your journal, your tuning records and your settings are stored only on your device, including the Home Screen widgets and the daily notification. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Google backup: if you have turned on backup in Android’s system settings, Android may back this data up to your own Google account, and may carry it over when you move to a new phone.
    • Cloud backup happens only when it can be end-to-end encrypted, which means your phone needs a screen lock. Android 8 makes no backup.
    • We can’t see these backups, and you can turn backup off in system settings at any time.
  • Read aloud: uses only the offline voices installed on your phone, on your device.
  • Chakra music: files built into the app; no connection needed. The media notification while something plays (chants included) stays on your device.

8. Changes

When this policy changes, this page and its effective date are updated. Before any new feature that sends data online (such as a daily guidance), this policy will be updated first.

アプリに入力したデータはあなたの端末に保存され、iCloud 同期をオンにした場合に限り、あなた自身の iCloud にも保存されます。収集も追跡もせず、広告もありません。

1. データは端末とあなた自身の iCloud に

誕生日帳の名前と誕生日、日記の天気・気分・シンクロ・メモ、チューニングの記録、各種設定は、あなたの端末（ホーム画面ウィジェットを含む）に保存され、私たちや第三者に送信されることはありません。

iCloud 同期（iPhone・iPad、初期設定はオフ）：「設定 › iCloud 同期」で「リアルタイム同期」を選ぶと、これらのデータは Apple の iCloud（CloudKit と iCloud キー値ストレージ）を通じて、あなたの Apple ID のプライベートな iCloud 領域に保存され、同じ Apple ID でサインインしているほかの端末と同期されます。このデータにアクセスできるのはあなただけで、私たちが見たり読んだりすることはできません。Apple のプライバシーポリシーが適用されます。通知のオン／オフなど、端末ごとの設定は同期されません。

同期中は、1 台で削除した内容がすべての端末から削除されます。「オフ」に戻すとこの端末は同期を停止しますが、iCloud にあるデータはあなたが削除するまで残ります。

2. アプリの通信先

  • iCloud（Apple）：iCloud 同期をオンにした場合のみ接続します。第 1 項をご覧ください。

  • sabee.space（アプリ 1.2 以前のバージョン）：これらのバージョンはポッドキャスト一覧を取得するために接続します。接続時にサーバーは IP アドレスなどの一般的な接続情報を受け取りますが、サービス提供のためだけに使い、あなたの識別や追跡には使いません。リクエストに名前・誕生日・日記の内容は含まれません。

1. 데이터는 기기와 본인의 iCloud에

생일부의 이름과 생일, 일기의 날씨·기분·동시성·메모, 조율 기록, 각종 설정은 기기(홈 화면 위젯 포함)에 저장되며, 저희나 제3자에게 전송되지 않습니다.

iCloud 동기화(iPhone·iPad, 기본값: 끔): 「설정 › iCloud 동기화」에서 「실시간 동기화」를 선택하면, 이 데이터는 Apple의 iCloud(CloudKit 및 iCloud 키-값 저장소)를 통해 본인 Apple ID의 비공개 iCloud 공간에 저장되고, 같은 Apple ID로 로그인한 다른 기기와 동기화됩니다. 이 데이터는 본인만 접근할 수 있으며, 저희는 보거나 읽을 수 없습니다. Apple의 개인정보 처리방침이 적용됩니다. 알림 켜기 여부처럼 기기별 설정은 동기화되지 않습니다.

동기화 중에는 한 기기에서 삭제한 내용이 모든 기기에서 삭제됩니다. 「끄기」로 바꾸면 이 기기는 동기화를 멈추며, 이미 iCloud에 있는 데이터는 직접 삭제할 때까지 남아 있습니다.

Quand la synchronisation est active, ce que vous supprimez sur un appareil est supprimé sur tous. Si vous revenez à « Désactivée », cet appareil cesse de se synchroniser ; les données déjà dans iCloud y restent jusqu'à ce que vous les supprimiez.
